for commercial purposes. Research groups, humanitarian organisations,
businesses and paid consultants can exercise those permissions without
requesting individual approval, subject to the licence terms.

and applicable notices, provide licence information, link to the source where
reasonably practicable, and indicate changes. Do not imply endorsement by
the creators. The licence does not require adaptations to use the same

## Scholarly citation

Please cite [Pillosu et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-1591)
and the version-specific dataset record when publishing research using PoFF.
Record the software version or commit and model checksum where relevant.
The paper citation is a scholarly request, not an additional condition of
CC BY 4.0. Machine-readable method citation is in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff).

No software or dataset DOI has yet been assigned in this package. The paper
DOI identifies the method and must not be presented as the dataset DOI.

## Scope

These data terms apply to the project's derived products explicitly released
under CC BY 4.0. They do not relicense third-party meteorological inputs,
dependencies, background maps, model files with separate terms or earlier
releases. The licence covers only applicable rights that the licensors can
grant; it does not create ownership of unprotected facts.

The Python code, example configuration and software documentation use
[Apache 2.0](LICENSE). Running the code does not by itself impose a licence on
someone else's outputs. The example configuration contains the publication
metadata for this project's products; other producers should supply their
own accurate attribution and licensing metadata.

The complete CC BY 4.0 terms include disclaimers of warranties and limitations
of liability. In particular, the licence does not provide a guarantee of
forecast accuracy or confer official warning status.
